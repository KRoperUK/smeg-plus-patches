# Mechanical

Drivetrain jobs that need the car in the air and the gearbox out. The 208 is
front-wheel drive, so the clutch is a gearbox-out job.

## Clutch replacement

Four procedures make the complete job. Read them in this order:

| Step | Procedure | Code | Manual page |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Engine-gearbox assembly — removing/refitting | `B1BG01BJP0` | 2344 (repeated at 2670) |
| 2 | Clutch tightening torques | `B2BB010NP0` | 2680 |
| 3 | Clutch mechanism — removing/refitting | `B2BG011FP0` | 2682 |
| 4 | Bleeding the hydraulic clutch control | `B2BI010EP0` | 2690 |

!!! warning "Gearbox-out job"

    Step 1 is the access job: the engine and gearbox come out as one assembly
    before the clutch is reachable. The manual's safety and cleanliness
    instructions are part of each sequence — read the whole range, not just the
    mechanism section. The airbag and pre-tensioner warnings on the
    [overview page](index.md) apply while the battery is disconnected and the
    car is raised.

### B1BG01BJP0 - Engine-gearbox assembly

Removing/refitting the engine and gearbox as one assembly. Page **2344**;
the same procedure is bookmarked again at page **2670**.

This is the access step for the clutch. It also covers the mountings and the
connections that have to be released before the assembly moves.

### B2BB010NP0 - Clutch tightening torques

Page **2680**. The torque table for the clutch fixings, used while the
mechanism is going back on.

### B2BG011FP0 - Clutch mechanism

Removing/refitting the clutch mechanism, pages **2682–2689**. The centre of the
job: friction plate, pressure plate and release bearing.

### B2BI010EP0 - Clutch bleeding

Page **2690**. Bleeding the hydraulic control after the mechanism is back in.
