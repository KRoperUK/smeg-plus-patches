# Body & fascia

Trim, the fascia stack and the big interior panels. Everything here is reached
from inside the car; none of it needs the head unit on a bench.

| # | Procedure | Code | Manual pages |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Multifunction screen | `C5FG01A6P0` | 1046, 1220, 1793 |
| 2 | Instrument panel | `C5FG01A7P0` | 1227, 1800 |
| 3 | Dashboard | `C5FG01AEP0` | 1826, 1852 (RHD) |
| 4 | Centre console | `C5FG01A8P0` | 1804 |
| 5 | Fascia air vents | `C5FG01AAP0` | 1055, 1813 |
| 6 | Fascia panel switches | `C5FG01ADP0` | 1063, 1821 |

The dashboard also has stripping/assembling procedures: `C5FS011AP0` at page
**1860** and `C5FS011BP0` at page **1865** for right-hand drive.

## C5FG01A6P0 - Multifunction screen

The SMEG+ display and the trim around it. Pages **1046, 1220, 1793** — the
three occurrences reach the same sequence.

Start here for a screen swap or to get behind the fascia. The display carries
LVDS and the touch/button connector; [Hardware](../HARDWARE.md) has the
connector details.

Related: [Hardware](../HARDWARE.md) · [Flashing](../FLASHING.md) (once the
trim is back together).

## C5FG01A7P0 - Instrument panel

Pages **1227, 1800**. The cluster is a separate unit from the centre display;
configuration entries are under [Electrical & audio](electrical.md#d6am01cnp0-instrument-panel-configuration).

## C5FG01AEP0 - Dashboard

Removal/refit page **1826**; page **1852** for right-hand drive.
Stripping/assembling is `C5FS011AP0` at page **1860** (`C5FS011BP0` at page
**1865** for right-hand drive).

The big job: everything in the fascia hangs off it. The airbag and
pre-tensioner warnings on the [overview page](index.md) apply here.

Related: [Hardware](../HARDWARE.md) (what sits behind it) ·
[Centre console](#c5fg01a8p0-centre-console) (the lower stack comes out first).

## C5FG01A8P0 - Centre console

Page **1804**. Gives access to the lower stack, the cupholders and the cable
run into the console.

## C5FG01AAP0 - Fascia air vents

Pages **1055, 1813**. The vents unclip; behind them is the top of the radio
stack.

## C5FG01ADP0 - Fascia panel switches

Pages **1063, 1821**. The switch strip below the vents (hazard, central
locking and adjacent controls).
